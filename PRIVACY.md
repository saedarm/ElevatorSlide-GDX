# Privacy Policy: Elevators & Laundry Chutes

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Elevators & Laundry Chutes is a single-device board game made by Sam (GitHub: saedarm).

**What the game collects:** nothing. The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no network access. It does not request the internet permission, so it cannot send anything off your phone.

**What the game stores on your phone:** your settings (music volume, sound volume, vibration, camera and animation options). They stay on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the game.

**Vibration:** the game asks for the vibration permission only to buzz the phone during play. You can turn this off in Settings.

**Children:** the game does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

**Changes:** if this ever changes, this page will be updated before the new version is released.

**Contact:** open an issue at https://github.com/saedarm/ElevatorSlide-GDX/issues
